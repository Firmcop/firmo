Questions

What people ask us.

Won't a container be hot inside?

Not when it is insulated and ventilated well. The standard spec lines walls and roof with 100 mm PIR board, and the cladding sits on battens so air moves behind it.

Do I need county approval?

In most counties, yes, as for any permanent building. We prepare the architectural and structural drawings, and can handle the application for you.

How long will it last?

The steel is blasted, primed and clad, so it is protected from the weather. It carries a [X]-year structural warranty, and finishes carry their makers' warranties.

Can I add units later?

Yes. The design is set up to join a second unit. Tell us at the start and we will place the plinths and services to suit.
